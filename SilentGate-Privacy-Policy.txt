SILENTGATE PRIVACY POLICY
=========================
Developer: SplojTech
Last updated: October 21, 2026

SilentGate is a call blocker and phone app. SilentGate is designed to keep your
call data on your own device. SilentGate has no user accounts, no advertising,
no analytics, no tracking, and does not request Internet access.

INFORMATION SILENTGATE USES ON YOUR DEVICE
------------------------------------------
• Contacts: used to determine whether an incoming caller is saved in your
  contacts. Contact data never leaves your device.
• Phone numbers and call events: used to screen calls, show caller names,
  display your blocked log and whitelist, and manage native call blocking.
• Call log: used to display recents and delete blocked-call entries from the
  phone call log.
• Local app database: stores blocked numbers, attempt timestamps, and
  whitelisted numbers on your device so Shield and Recents work.

SilentGate does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or upload personal data to
SplojTech or any third party. SilentGate does not have the Android INTERNET
permission.

DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
---------------------------
All SilentGate data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any
time by clearing SilentGate's app storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling
SilentGate. Numbers that were synced to Android's native block list can be
managed from Settings → Open system blocked numbers or removed in SilentGate
before uninstalling.

PERMISSIONS
-----------
SilentGate requests contacts, call log, phone, and notification permissions only
to perform the call-screening and dialer features described above. SilentGate
requires the Android Call Screening role and Default Phone app role to reject
calls before ringing and to provide the in-call experience.

CHILDREN
--------
SilentGate is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from children.
